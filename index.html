Breath Hold Trainer

Your personal apnea training companion — structured CO₂/O₂ tables, guided lung warmups, benchmark assessments, and land & pool workouts, all in one app.

Everything you need to train

From your first breath hold to a full custom training program.

Lung Warmups

Six guided breathing exercises — Box, Triangle, Resonance, Power Breathing, Gradual Static, and Breath Hold Ladder — to prepare your lungs and body before training.

Assessments

Establish your benchmark with Static Baseline, Dynamic Apnea, the BOLT Test, and Max Exhale — the numbers your training tables are built from.

CO₂ & O₂ Tables

Classic structured tables that scale to your max breath hold — shrinking recovery to build CO₂ tolerance, or climbing holds to push your O₂ efficiency.

Custom Tables

Build your own hold/recovery tables from a template or from scratch — drag the bars, tap to type exact values, and save your favorites to run anytime.

Land WOD Generator

Auto-generated bodyweight & sandbag workouts that weave in breath-hold work, tuned to your chosen time and difficulty.

Pool WOD Generator

Aquatic workouts built around underwater crossovers, bobs, and sandbag drills — real variety, every session.

Training Log & Progress

Every session tracked with a heatmap calendar, progress charts, and your personal records surfaced automatically.

H2O Game

A playful underwater mini-game to make practicing your breath hold a little more fun.

Update history

Every major release, straight from the changelog.

8.3.3 Aug 7, 2026

Minor fixes

Minor fixes.

8.3.2 Aug 7, 2026

Optional pre-test countdown

Added an optional 10-second get-ready countdown to BOLT, Max Exhale, Static Baseline, and Dynamic assessments — the same lead-in lung warmups already give you before a breath hold. Toggle it on per test from a picker right on the screen if you want a moment to prepare before the timer starts.

8.3.1 Aug 7, 2026

Manual max entry fix

Fixed a bug where entering your max breath hold manually wouldn't unlock your CO₂, O₂, and Custom Tables — previously only entries logged for today updated your live benchmark, so a backdated entry could silently leave training tables locked. Your benchmark now always reflects your most current known max.

8.3.0 Jul 22, 2026

Custom Tables

Build and save your own custom breath-hold training tables — pick a template, drag to adjust hold and recovery times, and run it like any other table. CO₂, O₂, and Custom Tables now live together under one Training Tables menu. Personal records are now called out right in your training log, charts, and after every session.

8.2.0 Jul 22, 2026

Smarter workout generators

Land and Pool WOD difficulty now actually changes your workout — harder settings mean more breath-hold work and tougher movements, easier settings dial it back. Land WODs also hit your target time more accurately, and both generators give more variety between rounds. Manual max entries can be backdated and show up properly in your log and progress charts.

Train safely

Breath hold training carries real risk, especially in water. Never train breath holds alone, always have a trained buddy present for any water-based session, and consult a healthcare professional before starting. Breath Hold Trainer includes safety guidance throughout the app — please read and follow it.
