Everything you need to train

From your first breath hold to a full custom training program.

🫁

Lung Warmups

Six guided breathing exercises — Box, Triangle, Resonance, Power Breathing, Gradual Static, and Breath Hold Ladder — to prepare your lungs and body before training.

📋

Assessments

Establish your benchmark with Static Baseline, Dynamic Apnea, the BOLT Test, and Max Exhale — the numbers your training tables are built from.

⏱️

CO₂ & O₂ Tables

Classic structured tables that scale to your max breath hold — shrinking recovery to build CO₂ tolerance, or climbing holds to push your O₂ efficiency.

🎛️

Custom Tables

Build your own hold/recovery tables from a template or from scratch — drag the bars, tap to type exact values, and save your favorites to run anytime.

🏔️

Land WOD Generator

Auto-generated bodyweight & sandbag workouts that weave in breath-hold work, tuned to your chosen time and difficulty.

🏊

Pool WOD Generator

Aquatic workouts built around underwater crossovers, bobs, and sandbag drills — real variety, every session.

📈

Training Log & Progress

Every session tracked with a heatmap calendar, progress charts, and your personal records surfaced automatically.

🎮

H2O Game

A playful underwater mini-game to make practicing your breath hold a little more fun.

Train safely

Breath hold training carries real risk, especially in water. Never train breath holds alone, always have a trained buddy present for any water-based session, and consult a healthcare professional before starting. Breath Hold Trainer includes safety guidance throughout the app — please read and follow it.
